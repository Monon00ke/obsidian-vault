**Slide 2: The Problem** Plastic pollution is everywhere. Every year we throw away **1 billion** plastic toothbrushes. They go to landfill and stay there for **400+ years**. This plastic pollutes the **soil**, kills useful bacteria and makes the land bad for plants. It also hurts animals and the whole environment.

(Фото: свалка с пластиком, грязная земля)

---

**Slide 3: Our Product** We chose **bamboo toothbrush + toothpaste tablets**. It is a simple everyday thing. The toothbrush is made from bamboo. Toothpaste is in small tablets — no plastic tube.

(Фото продукта)

---

**Slide 4: How it works**

- Handle = bamboo (grows very fast, no chemicals)
- Bristles = special material that can break down
- Toothpaste tablets = natural (baking soda, coconut oil, mint)

After use: You can put the toothbrush in compost. It disappears in **6–24 months** and turns into healthy soil. No plastic in the ground!

---

**Slide 5: Advantages (Pros)**

- Helps the soil (becomes compost)
- Bamboo is natural and grows quickly
- Zero plastic waste
- No harmful chemicals
- Good for the whole environment
- Looks nice and natural

---

**Slide 6: Disadvantages (Cons) + Comparison**

**Cons:**

- Costs 2–3 times more

- Bristles wear out faster

**Slide 7: Our opinion** We think this is a good and easy way to help the environment. It is not perfect and a bit expensive, but small changes like this are important. If many people use bamboo toothbrushes, we will have cleaner soil and better nature.
